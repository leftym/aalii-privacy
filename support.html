Common questions

Where is my game saved?
On your phone, inside the app. The game saves after every season and when you close it. There are no accounts and nothing is stored online.
How do I move a reign to another phone?
In Settings, tap Back up this reign to copy your save as text, then use Load a saved game on the other phone and paste it in.
Does it need the internet?
No. Aliʻi plays fully offline, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
How do I start in another age?
Tap New reign and choose When: around 1500 (ʻUmi’s rise), 1620, or 1782 (Kamehameha’s wars).
The text is too small or too large.
Settings has a text size control, and light and dark themes.
